Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 05, 2026

1. Definitions

In this Privacy Policy, key terms are defined as follows:

  • Personal Data: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • User: The subject or visitor interacting with our portfolio and digital services.
  • Service: Our complete digital ecosystem including this website and integrated communication features.

2. Information We Collect

We collect data through direct user interaction and automated tracking technologies:

Direct Information

WhatsApp phone numbers, names, email addresses, and message contents provided during direct communication.

Automated Information

IP addresses, browser types, general geographic locations, and basic site navigation history.

3. Legal Basis and Usage

Your data is processed strictly for the following operational purposes:

  • Providing consultation and inquiry services through WhatsApp API integration.
  • Optimizing website performance and user experience through anonymous analytics.
  • Preventing illegal activities, spam, or platform security breaches.
  • Complying with applicable legal regulations within the Republic of Indonesia.

4. Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent personal data to third parties. Data sharing is strictly restricted to essential technical infrastructure providers:

  • WhatsApp Inc.: Used as your chosen secure communication channel.
  • Cloud Hosting Providers: For encrypted data storage and high-speed website delivery.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard 256-bit SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) encryption for all data exchanges. Communication records are stored securely for up to 2 years or as long as necessary for administrative resolution, after which they are safely purged or anonymized.

6. Your Rights

In accordance with data privacy standards, you maintain full control over your information:

Access & Portability

You have the right to request a digital copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Right to Rectification

You can request updates or corrections if any of your information is inaccurate.

Right to Erasure

You may request the permanent deletion of your communication records from our database.

7. Children's Privacy

Our platform is not directed at individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we discover that data has been collected inadvertently, we will take immediate steps to delete such information.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant updates will be reflected by the revised date at the top of this page. Your continued use of our services following any changes signifies your agreement to the updated policy.
